Questions

Good to know.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. We agree a clear schedule with you in the first conversation, so you know what to expect before work begins.

Can you work with us from anywhere in Kenya?

Yes. We work over WhatsApp, calls and email, so your location is not a barrier.

Can you update our site after it launches?

Yes. Ongoing support is part of what we do, from small content updates to new sections and features.

Can the site show payment and contact details?

We can add M-Pesa payment details, WhatsApp contact buttons, Google Maps directions and contact forms, depending on what your organisation needs.

Do you help with domain names and hosting?

Yes. We will guide you through choosing and setting up a domain and hosting that fit your budget.
